Driver pay per trip

When you tap Pay driver on a delivered order, the amount starts at this and you can change it before sending. Money goes from your PayPal balance to the driver's PayPal or Venmo, or from Branch to the driver's Branch wallet.
To stop auto pay for one driver only, uncheck Auto pay on that driver under Restaurants and drivers.
Auto pay sends the trip's suggested pay by PayPal, Venmo or Branch once the customer's payment is settled. Drivers paid by check, drivers with auto pay off, drivers with nothing on file, and failed payments are left for you on the order.

Dispatch shows a quiet banner as soon as an order goes to the kitchen or a driver. Once these minutes pass without an accept, dispatch flashes and chimes every 5 seconds until it is accepted.

Stacking by location

A busy driver gets another order only when it's from the same or a nearby restaurant, they haven't picked anything up yet, they're under their stack limit, and the drop-off is on their way. Their stops are then put in the shortest drop-off order. Otherwise orders go to the next free driver in turn.
The short label dispatch puts on an order and the driver sees on the stop, e.g. Online, App, Phone call, Third party.
