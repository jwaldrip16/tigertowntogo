Late accept warnings

Dispatch shows a quiet banner as soon as an order goes to the kitchen or a driver. Once these minutes pass without an accept, dispatch flashes and chimes every 5 seconds until it is accepted.

Driver schedule

Before that the driver sits in Unavailable and the driver app says they are not scheduled. They stay Scheduled until the shift ends, or longer while they are still online.

Delivery fees

On: auto dispatch hands the order to a driver the moment you tap Send to kitchen, so they head over while the food cooks. Off: the driver is assigned once the kitchen accepts and sets a prep time (or you mark a called-in order Ordering in process).
Stops phones and tablets from going to sleep while the app is open, so new orders, alerts and GPS keep coming in. Each device can still switch it off with the Keep screen awake button.

Stacking by location

A busy driver gets another order only when it's from the same or a nearby restaurant, they haven't picked anything up yet, they're under their stack limit, and the drop-off is on their way. Their stops are then put in the shortest drop-off order. Otherwise orders go to the next free driver in turn.
The short label dispatch puts on an order and the driver sees on the stop, e.g. Online, App, Phone call, Third party.
