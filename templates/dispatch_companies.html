Companies in the shared apps

These are the companies workers can pick in the Fleet Foot Driver and Fleet Foot Kitchen apps. The code is what you give their drivers and restaurants. Turn off "Show in list" to make a company reachable by code only. Only developer accounts see this page.

To add a separate company, deploy its own copy of the app on Railway (its own database), then add it here with its own web address. It shows as "Separate platform": its dispatchers, drivers and restaurants sign in on its own site and can't see or reach any other company. A brand's address on this app (like Bulldawg Food or Crimson To Go) shows as "This platform" and shares this dispatch. Listed companies also appear on the main dispatch sign-in page so dispatchers can pick the company they work for.
