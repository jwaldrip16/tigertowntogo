Card payments

Tiger Town To Go is card only. Customers pay on Stripe's own page, so no card number ever reaches this app or its database. Nothing charges until you switch it on below.

Status {{ 'live' if on else 'off' }}

Setting it up

  1. Make a Stripe account, then open Developers, API keys, and copy both keys above.
  2. In Stripe, open Developers, Webhooks, add an endpoint pointed at {{ hook }} and subscribe it to checkout.session.completed. Copy the signing secret it gives you into the box above.
  3. Test with the test keys first: card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future date, any CVC.

How each order gets paid

  • Website order. The customer pays on Stripe before the order reaches you. Nothing shows on the board or in the kitchen until the payment lands.
  • Call-in. Place the order, then either text the customer a card link from the order card, or key their card into Stripe's virtual terminal and hit Paid.
  • Tip at the door. If the customer left no tip online, the driver's phone asks for one and takes a signature, and the tip charges to the same card.
  • Refunds. Open any paid order on the board and use Refund, all of it or part.
